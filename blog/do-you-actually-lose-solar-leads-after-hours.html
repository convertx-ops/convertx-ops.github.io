Do you actually lose solar leads after hours? (the math you're avoiding)

I am Rihan, founder of ConvertX Ops. I understand that local business owners are often skeptical about the value of 24/7 AI voice agents for their operations. Today, I want to address a common concern: do you actually lose solar leads after hours? The answer is yes, you can indeed lose leads after hours, and the math behind it can be illuminating.

Let's consider a scenario where a local solar company generates 1000 leads per month. On average, let's assume they get 20 calls per day, with a response rate of 10%, meaning they get 2 qualified leads per day. Assuming it takes 3 days to qualify a prospect, that means they would have about 20 qualified leads per week.

Now, if their business operates 24/7 and their AI agents are available 24/7, they could manage 10 calls per hour, leading to 200 leads per week. This is a significant increase from the 20 qualified leads they would get if their AI agents were only available during business hours.

Moreover, the value of these leads doesn't diminish just because it's after hours. In fact, after-hours calls often represent a higher conversion rate since the prospects are generally more interested and ready to commit. This could lead to more immediate bookings and a better ROI for the business.

Furthermore, by having a 24/7 AI service, you ensure that you don't miss out on these potential high-value appointments, thereby maintaining a steady stream of valuable leads and clients. This proactive approach can significantly reduce the risk of losing leads and enhance your overall lead conversion rates.
