Why I built ConvertX Ops - founder Rihan Pathan's story

I am Rihan, founder of ConvertX Ops. Our mission is to empower local service businesses by building 24/7 AI voice agents that streamline their appointment booking process. I am confident that ConvertX Ops will revolutionize how local businesses interact with their potential customers. Here’s why I built ConvertX Ops from the perspective of a local business owner.

I started ConvertX Ops because I witnessed firsthand the challenges that local businesses face when it comes to customer service. The high volume of calls, the need to qualify prospects manually, and the complexity of coordinating appointments all lead to inefficiencies and missed opportunities. It was clear to me that there had to be a better way.

Our AI voice agents are designed to handle every inbound call, instantly qualifying prospects based on predefined criteria. The agents can screen calls, prioritize them, and even book appointments right from the call, all without the need for human intervention. This not only saves time for our clients but also ensures a seamless customer experience.

For example, consider a plumbing company. With ConvertX Ops, they can set up their criteria to automatically route calls based on the customer’s location, urgency of the issue, and the time of day. The AI agents can handle everything from basic maintenance to emergency repairs, saving the company valuable time and resources.

Moreover, our AI agents are scalable. As the business grows, so does the capability of the AI system. This is crucial for businesses that experience significant growth, needing to handle more calls and appointments. The scalability of our solution means local businesses can adapt without a significant upfront build or complex integration process.
