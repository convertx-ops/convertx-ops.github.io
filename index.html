Questions, Answered

Does it replace my existing phone system?

No. ConvertX connects to your current business phone line. Your team keeps their numbers and handsets.

Can it qualify leads using my rules?

Yes. You set the criteria — service type, area, timeline, budget — and the agent screens against them.

Can it book directly into my calendar?

Yes. Confirmed appointments land on your calendar automatically, with the caller's details attached.

Can calls be transferred to my team?

Yes. When a call needs a human, the agent can route it to the right person with context.

Can I customize what the agent says?

Yes. The agent is configured around your business, services, and tone.

What happens if the AI can't answer something?

It qualifies what it can and routes the rest to your team with a summary — nothing is dropped.

How long does setup take?

Setup starts with a live demo on your real enquiry flow. Full configuration is scoped with you.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There is no long lock-in. You can stop the service if it isn't recovering missed opportunities.

What does implementation include?

Connection to your phone line and calendar, agent configuration to your rules, and a live demo before go-live.
